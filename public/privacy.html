Privacy Policy

Updated 15 September 2026

This policy describes how logo2logo handles information when you use logo2logo.vercel.app. For privacy requests, contact korbinzhao@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account information: your sign-in provider, the account identifier it supplies, your display name and session records. Google or GitHub handles your password; logo2logo does not receive it.
  • Creative work: your brand brief, prompts, chosen references, generation settings, generated images, revisions and associated history and job records.
  • Billing information: purchased packs, payment references, payment and refund status, and your credit ledger. PayPal collects the contact, billing and payment details required by its checkout. Our application does not store full card numbers or card security codes.
  • Support and technical information: messages you send us and operational information such as requests, error records, timestamps and IP addresses processed by the hosting and payment services.

Why we use this information

We process information to authenticate you, generate and deliver images, retain your design history, account for credits and payments, resolve support requests, prevent misuse and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Depending on the laws that apply, these activities rely on providing the requested service, legitimate operational and security interests, legal obligations, or consent where it is required.

Service providers and sharing

The website is hosted by Vercel. Google and GitHub provide the available sign-in services, and PayPal provides checkout and payment processing. Database and image-storage services hold account and design records; AI processing providers receive the brief, prompts and any reference or source image needed for planning, generation, refinement or visual review. These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

The cloud implementation uses Turso for account and job records and supports protected image storage such as Cloudinary. AI services used by the application include OpenCode Go and fal, with providers depending on the deployed configuration. Contact us for the current provider details relevant to your work. Provider processing is subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices; do not submit information you do not want processed for the requested task.

Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with applicable law or protect the service and its users. We do not offer your private design history as a public gallery.

Cookies and browser storage

We use necessary session and sign-in cookies to keep you logged in and complete authentication. Login sessions expire after 30 days unless ended earlier. Browser storage remembers preferences such as language and appearance and may retain in-progress interface state. You can clear this storage in your browser; doing so may sign you out or reset preferences.

Retention and deletion

Account, design and credit records are retained to provide account history and the service. Retention also depends on unresolved support issues, security needs and applicable accounting or legal obligations. There is no promise of automatic deletion after a fixed period.

You can request access, correction or deletion by email. We may need to verify account ownership. Some billing or security records may need to be retained where legally required or necessary to resolve a dispute. Clearing browser storage or signing out does not delete server-side records.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict or object to processing of your information, withdraw consent where applicable, or complain to a relevant data protection authority. Send requests to the contact above so we can assess and respond under the applicable rules.

Updates

We will update this page when our data practices change. The revision date appears above. For questions about this policy or a particular provider, email our support address.
