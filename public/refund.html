Refund & Cancellation Policy

Updated 15 September 2026

For purchases from logo2logo at logo2logo.vercel.app, all refund requests are reviewed manually through korbinzhao@gmail.com.

How to request a refund

Email us with your PayPal transaction or receipt reference, the email used at checkout, the purchased credit pack and the reason for your request. Do not send full card numbers, card security codes, passwords or bank login details.

We review each request individually, including the payment record, credit usage and any delivery or technical issue. We do not promise a fixed discretionary refund eligibility window or a fixed review or payment-processing time. Sending a request does not by itself confirm approval. We will communicate the outcome by email.

Approved refunds

Approved payment refunds are processed through PayPal. The time before funds appear depends on PayPal, the payment method and the financial institution. Credits associated with an approved refund are reversed in your logo2logo account; a pending request does not itself reverse credits.

Technical credit returns

A technical generation failure that produces no result returns the affected usage credit automatically. This restores a credit to your account and is not a refund of the original payment. Delivered images use credits even when a visual check flags the result. Contact us if you believe credits were charged incorrectly or you are dissatisfied with the service.

No recurring subscription

Our credit packs are one-time purchases and do not renew automatically. There is no subscription or recurring charge to cancel. You can stop using the service at any time; account deletion and payment refunds are separate requests.

Consumer rights

This policy does not limit any refund, cancellation or other remedy you are entitled to under applicable law or the applicable payment terms. Mandatory rights take precedence over our discretionary review process.
